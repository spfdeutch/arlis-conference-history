# ARLIS/NA Conference History

An interactive visualization of the Art Libraries Society of North America (ARLIS/NA) annual conference attendance and membership data from 1972 to 2026.

## About

This project presents 54 years of ARLIS/NA conference history as an interactive line chart, showing:

- **Conference attendance** at each annual meeting
- **Membership figures** over the same period
- Averaged and projected data points clearly distinguished from confirmed figures

Key highlights include the society's growth from 10 attendees at the inaugural 1972 Chicago conference to a peak of 908 at the 2018 New York City meeting, alongside membership that reached a high of 1,391 in 1993.

## Data Notes

- 1976–1978 and 1981 attendance figures are averages based on Executive Board reports
- 1999–2001 and 2004–2005 attendance figures are averages based on Executive Board reports
- 2020 conference was cancelled (St. Louis)
- 2021 conference was held virtually
- 2025 conference was held virtually; figures are year-to-date
- 2026 Montreal registration figure is as of April 18, 2026
